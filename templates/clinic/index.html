Patient information

Before you come in

Five minutes of preparation saves a second trip. Please read the points that apply to your visit.

What to bring

  • Previous prescriptions, even old ones
  • Any reports from the last two years
  • A written list of the medicines you take, with doses
  • Aadhaar or any photo identity card
  • Insurance or Ayushman card, if you have one
  • Spectacles, so you can read the prescription

Before a blood test

  • Fast for ten to twelve hours for sugar and lipid tests
  • Plain water is allowed and encouraged
  • Take your regular medicines unless the doctor says otherwise
  • Come between 7:00 and 11:00 am with the doctor’s slip
  • Tell the technician if you feel faint at the sight of blood

Fees and payment

  • First consultation ₹400
  • Follow-up within fifteen days ₹200
  • Dressing or injection ₹150
  • Tests are charged at the rates listed on this page
  • Cash and UPI accepted; receipt given every time
