The studio

Nine chairs, one very particular owner.

Aarna opened above Shreeji Complex in 2016 with two chairs and a borrowed steamer. Ritika still cuts here six days a week, still argues with suppliers about wax quality, and still writes every client's colour formula in a notebook so the shade is the same next time.

We keep the room quiet, the tools sterilised, and the price list on the wall. If you are not sure what you want, send us a photo on WhatsApp and we will tell you honestly what will suit you.

No client should have to ask what something costs after it has already been done. Ritika Sharma · Founder
