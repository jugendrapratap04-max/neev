The floor

Everything you need. Nothing you'll never use.

Two floors, laid out so the strength area never fights the cardio area. Equipment is wiped down between batches and serviced every month.

Strength zone

Four power racks, two Olympic platforms, dumbbells to 50 kg and calibrated plates for the barbell lifts.

Cardio & conditioning

Treadmills, air bikes, rowers and a 20-metre turf strip for sled pushes and sprint work.

Air-conditioned hall

The group class hall is fully air-conditioned with a sprung floor for yoga, Zumba and boxing rounds.

Changing rooms

Separate men's and women's changing rooms with hot-water showers, running through the winter months.

Lockers & parking

Day lockers for every member and covered two-wheeler parking right outside the entrance.

Written training plan

Every member starts with a fitness assessment and leaves with a four-week plan on paper, reviewed monthly.
